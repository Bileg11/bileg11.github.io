Renting an apartment in Shanghai

Finding a place is hard without Chinese and without knowing the city. We find the listings, view them with you in person, and sit through the lease signing to explain it. You can also choose from Mongolia and move in the day you land.

Fee 1,000 CNY · one-time

Whether you are here to study or to work, renting in Shanghai does not work the way it does in Mongolia. Landlords speak only Chinese, the lease is written in Chinese, and there is an agency commission on top. If you do not know about these in advance, the costs arrive as a surprise.

💰 How the payments split

Paid to LFS
1,000 ¥
A one-time fee. Everything listed above is covered.
  • Deposit up front 500 ¥
  • After the lease is signed 500 ¥

The fee is non-refundable.

Paid to the apartment — separately
3 payments up front
These are not part of the LFS fee of 1,000 CNY. They go directly to the landlord and the agency.
  • 1 month rent 2,000–4,000 ¥
  • 1 month security deposit equal to the rent
  • Agency commission 10% of total rent

From the second month you pay only the rent.

❓ Common questions

Why is there an agency commission?

Most apartments in Shanghai are let through an agency. The commission is 10% of the total rent over the lease — for example, an apartment at 3,000 CNY a month taken for 10 months means 10% of 30,000 CNY, so 3,000 CNY. It is separate from the LFS fee and goes straight to the agency.

Do I get the deposit back?

Yes. The deposit is returned when the lease ends and you hand the apartment back, provided there is no damage and everything has been paid.

Can I sign a lease without a visa?

Yes. If you are not a student, a passport is enough. Mongolian citizens can enter China visa-free for up to 30 days — the visa-free rules are here.

How many apartments do we see in a day?

Two or three in one day. If nothing suitable comes up that day, we agree the price for a second day of viewings.

When do I pay the fee?

500 CNY before the work starts, and the remaining 500 CNY once the lease has been signed. The fee is non-refundable.

Can I choose the apartment before I arrive?

Yes. We visit and show you the place by photo and live video. Once you pick one, the Chinese agency holds it for 5-7 days. You sign and move in the day you land.

What if something goes wrong after signing?

Get in touch. Talking to the landlord, repairs, a dispute over payments — we help with whatever we can after the lease is signed. The relationship does not end when the fee is paid.

Who pays for water and electricity?

The tenant pays for water, electricity, internet and gas. They are not included in the monthly rent.
